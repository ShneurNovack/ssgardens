Our Mission

Every Jewish family deserves a sacred and respectful resting place rooted in tradition — regardless of affiliation, regardless of membership.

Shaarei Shamayim Gardens exists to serve the entire Jacksonville Jewish community. We welcome every family seeking a dignified, traditional burial — without barriers of synagogue membership or denomination.

Conveniently located on Jacksonville's Southside, our gardens are surrounded by natural beauty and meticulously maintained grounds, offering a tranquil environment that reflects honor, reverence, and continuity.

Halachic Standards
All arrangements and standards are upheld in accordance with the highest rabbinical guidelines for members of the Jewish faith, as determined by the Chief Rabbinate of Israel.

A Note from Tradition
סְגֻלָּה
— Segulah · A Spiritual Merit —

In our tradition, to arrange one's resting place in advance is considered a segulah — a spiritual merit for long life. By acting with foresight and dignity for those who come after us, we honor a wisdom passed down through generations.

Planning is not an end. It is an expression of love.
